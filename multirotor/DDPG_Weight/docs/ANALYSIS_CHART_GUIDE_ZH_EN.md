## Core Charts / 核心图表

### `episode_performance_summary.png` / Episode综合表现总览图

中文：展示每个 episode 的最终奖励、最大扫描比例、最小全局平均熵值。用于判断是否出现收敛趋势。

English: Shows episode-level final reward, max scan ratio, and minimum global average entropy. Use it as the main convergence chart.

### `reset_reason_rolling_ratio.png` / 重置原因滚动占比图

中文：展示最近 20 个 episode 内的重置原因占比变化。用于判断后期是否以正常达到时长上限结束为主。

English: Shows the rolling ratio of reset reasons. Use it to verify whether late training is dominated by healthy timeouts instead of collisions.

### `collision_hotspots_xy.png` / 碰撞热点分布图（XY）

中文：展示碰撞点在 XY 平面上的分布。用于识别固定障碍物热点、路径瓶颈区。

English: Shows collision positions on the XY plane. Use it to identify repeated obstacle hotspots and bottleneck areas.

### `collision_object_breakdown.png` / 碰撞对象统计图

中文：统计高频碰撞对象名称。用于判断主要失败源是哪个物体。

English: Counts frequent collision object names. Use it to locate the dominant failure source.

### `algorithm_weights_stability.png` / 权重稳定性分析图

中文：上图为各权重的滚动均值，下图为滚动标准差。用于判断权重是否稳定、是否仍在贴边或剧烈抖动。

English: Shows rolling mean and rolling std of adaptive weights. Use it to inspect stability, edge saturation, and oscillation.

### `best_vs_recent_trajectory_comparison.png` / 最优与最近轨迹对比图

中文：对比当前最优 episode 与最近 episode 的 XY 轨迹。用于判断近期策略是否在接近最优表现。

English: Compares the XY trajectories of the best episode and the latest episode. Use it to see whether recent behavior is approaching the best-known behavior.

## Common Misreadings / 常见误读

### Reward rises but scan ratio does not

中文：不能只看 reward，必须同时看扫描比例和熵值。

English: Do not judge learning quality from reward alone; always check scan ratio and entropy together.

### Timeout is not always a bad ending

中文：在当前任务中，`达到时长上限` 往往代表 episode 跑满。如果奖励和扫描比例较高，通常是健康结束。

English: In the current setup, timeout often means the episode survived to its maximum horizon. With high reward and scan ratio, it is usually a healthy ending.

### A single best episode does not mean convergence

中文：单次高分不代表收敛，要看最近 20 轮的平均表现是否提升并稳定。

English: One strong episode does not mean convergence. Check whether the last 20 episodes improve and stay stable.

中文：日常排查先看核心图，再看辅助图。如果目标是定位碰撞问题，优先看碰撞热点、碰撞对象统计和代表性轨迹图。

English: Start with the core charts. Then use the supporting charts for drill-down. For collision diagnosis, prioritize collision hotspots, collision-object breakdown, and representative trajectories.
